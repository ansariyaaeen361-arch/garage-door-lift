Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Garage Door Lift ("we", "us") operates this website as a product catalog for residential garage doors. This page explains what information we collect through the site and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you submit a quote request — through the Contact page or the Door Builder tool — we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, project details (address, city, state, country, postal code where given), and the door configuration you selected. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect a password or any account credentials.

How we use it

Contact and project information submitted through a quote request is used only to respond to that specific request — to follow up with pricing, availability, and next steps for your project. We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

Quote request records are retained only as long as needed to respond to the request and for our own basic business records.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want a quote request removed from our records, reach out through the Contact page.
